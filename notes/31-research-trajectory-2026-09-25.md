### Genuine TM irregularity, beyond a positive certificate checker

**Current result.**  `notes/28-discord-catchup-2026-09-23.md` records Shawn Ligocki's September 14 observation that TM regularity had not been formalized in any proof system and was a Rocq/Lean challenge; `notes/29-bb-collatz-brief-2026-09-23.md:106` retains it as option 4.  The bbchallenge wiki defines irregularity as absence of a regular closed tape language omitting halting configurations: https://wiki.bbchallenge.org/wiki/Irregular_Turing_Machine .  Its general closed-set criterion has entrance, closure, and nonhalting conditions: https://wiki.bbchallenge.org/wiki/Closed_Set .  Trevor already has TM step and reachability machinery in `lean/Collatz/BB.lean` and `lean/Collatz/BB/Multistep.lean`, plus a completed #494 nonhalting port at `lean/Collatz/BB33_494.lean:675`.

**Missing theorem.**  For a named known nonhalting machine, prove **no regular language** can both be closed under its TM operations and exclude halting configurations while containing the blank orbit.  This universally quantifies over all finite automata, so it is an obstruction theorem about the decider class.  A soundness theorem taking a *positive* regular-language certificate to nonhalting is an initial formalization step, but it cannot be reported as formalized irregularity and is not the ranked result.

**Mechanism and cheap test.**  Read the community's exact regular-CTL definition, then isolate an obstruction that defeats an *arbitrary* finite automaton, such as a pumping/Myhill–Nerode family of reachable configurations forcing a halt configuration into any closed regular overapproximation.  Try that family on a named exemplar, Finned #3 or Skelet 17, before building the Lean language layer.  A positive certificate checker can test semantics but is insufficient as the deliverable.  Feasibility ~10% for genuine irregularity on a near-term research push; community value high if the quantified obstruction closes.  It would formalize a new kind of impossibility result, not itself prove BB(6).

These are separate arithmetic and correspondence gaps.

**Missing theorem.**  Reachable orbit cannot hit the arithmetic halt band, especially the two V6 cascades, **and** V6 or Shawn macrostate simulation matches the TM.  Closing just `sim` formalizes a known reduction; closing just V6's derived `no_halt` is already done and does not close Bigfoot.

**Mechanism and cheap test.**  For arithmetic, use the 9-rule V6 recurrence to check a proposed invariant measuring recovery between `k` decrements, with state `(pat,a,b mod 12,k)` and explicit attention to the D-W cascade.  Run finite counterexample search on the proposal and negative control states, then attempt inductive preservation.  Avoid new low-dimensional Lyapunov guesses without checking `notes/18` and the existing 11-clause `InvB`.  For correspondence, first prove a small finite macrostep sample and identify one uniform loop invariant.  Mathematical success would settle Bigfoot, one of the BB(3,3) cryptids, and could help determine S(3,3) after the other holdouts.  Feasibility in a near-term session low (~5–10%).  Novelty high if arithmetic gap closes; correspondence alone is useful but known mathematics.

### Wily Coyote #531 sweep-language model (exploratory)

**Current result.**  `notes/20-10M-bootstrap-burn-results.md` has 10M macro loops: 531 left bootstrap was one observed word, right bootstrap produced 4,531 distinct words across 11,694 sampled peak reversals; novelty slowed but had not saturated.  `notes/21-bootstrap-language-structure.md` finds a common suffix backbone and recurrent high-frequency words, unlike 397's near-unique prefix growth.  `lean/Collatz/WilyCoyote/SweepPDA.lean` is only a trivial realization; the Wily hypothesis remains open.  **Correction:** `notes/21` says observed max word length 23 means words live in a globally bounded trie.  A finite burn proves only an observed maximum, so the inferred finite global state bound is unsupported; `notes/13` had already refuted a different bounded-length hypothesis.

**Missing theorem.**  A finite grammar/automaton or other inductive description of all reachable right bootstrap words, together with a transition invariant excluding halt.  Observed recurrent structure does not prove that the language saturates.

**Mechanism and cheap test.**  Infer a minimal grammar from the reversal data, then hold out a later segment and seek a counterexample to *closure under one actual macro transition*.  A held-out simulation can falsify a grammar cheaply, but cannot prove global finiteness.  Even a correct grammar would still require a no-halt invariant.  Novelty could be substantial for a BB(3,3) holdout; feasibility low (~10%).  Fat Coyote #397 is a worse immediate target given 99.6% unique peak words in the sampled run, though even that statistic is not proof of unbounded complexity.

### PA independence via state-count compression (a distant separate program, not evidence about BB(6))

**Current result.**  The bbchallenge logical-independence ledger gives a **372-state** explicit PA-independent machine attributed to Matthew House (February 2026), and states Aaronson's `N_PA ≤ 10` as a conjecture: https://wiki.bbchallenge.org/wiki/Independence_from_ZFC .  The source code is `https://github.com/LegionMammal978/turing_machine_explorer/blob/main/pa.py`.  The wiki notes its independence-machine table has not been formally verified.  Trevor's BB repo has no apparent PA proof-enumerator/compiler project, and the Goodstein independence repo is a different, retired formalization route.  No local evidence points to a compression mechanism approaching six states.

**Missing theorem.**  A concrete n-state machine with a verified simulation of proof enumeration for a sound theory, a proof that halting is equivalent to inconsistency, and a state count meaningfully below 372.  Even an explicit six-state PA-independent nonhalting machine would show `N_PA ≤ 6` under the relevant consistency assumptions; it would not prove BB(6) has an exact value.  Conversely BB(6)'s current cryptids have no demonstrated PA independence.

**Mechanism and cheap test.**  Audit House's generator for state contributions by subsystem, then replace exactly one major component and obtain a **measured** count reduction with behavior-preserving test vectors or a compiler-correctness argument.  Without an order-of-magnitude compression path, do not rank it in the September research top ten.  Historical interest high; near-term feasibility of reaching six states negligible.
